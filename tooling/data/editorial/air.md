# JetBrains Air — Editorial Seed

Voice: forward-looking, agentic-development focused, enterprise-credible. Air is
JetBrains' **agentic development environment** for orchestrating fleets of
background agents — a place to launch, watch, and steer many agents working in
parallel, rather than a single in-editor assistant.

## Sections to cover (roughly)

1. **Getting Started** (s1, ⚡) — what Air is, who it's for, how to access, first run / quick start.
2. **Agentic Workflow** (s2, 🌬️) — launching agents, the fleet view, parallel background work.
3. **Steering Agents** (s5, 🕹️) — sending messages, interrupting, approvals, reviewing output.
4. **Worktrees & Isolation** (s6, 📁) — isolated workspaces per agent, avoiding conflicts.
5. **Tools & MCP** (s7, 🔌) — connected tools/data sources, permissions and approvals.
6. **Models** (s8, 🧠) — model selection for agents, defaults.
7. **Tasks & Tracking** (s9, 📋) — structuring work, task lists, progress, outcomes.
8. **Integrations** (s10, 🔗) — git/VCS, IDEs, CI surfaces.
9. **Controls & Governance** (s12, 🏢) — permissions, audit, safe autonomy.
10. **Pro Tips** (s3, 💡) — scope work clearly, verify before trusting, keep humans on the approval path.

## Pro tips flavor
Fan out independent work to multiple agents; keep each task bounded and verifiable;
review and steer rather than fire-and-forget; keep approvals on for sensitive actions.

## Note
Air is newer and evolving — rely strictly on the official docs for feature names
and availability. End with an "Updated" row noting the docs scrape date.
